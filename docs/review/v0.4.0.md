# Review packet — v0.4.0

The visualiser plays to a plan of the track. This is what that means, how it
was checked, where a first attempt went wrong, and what is still not covered.

## What changed

**Foresight.** While the visualiser is open, a second, silent mpv measures the
track playing and the next one ahead of playback: the whole sound as the live
filter measures it, and five bands of a mono mix. The stream address is sent
over that mpv's socket and never on its command line. From the bands the
visualiser builds a plan: a beat grid, bars, kicks, snares, hats, softer
notes, sections, builds, drops, whether the track has a steady beat at all,
and its character.

**Playing to the plan.** With a plan, the beat, the kicks and the mood are the
plan's. Kicks are set off on the beat grid, on the first frame at or after the
beat. A drop, or a section where the kicks start or stop, is cut to in a third
of a second, and a stretch without kicks is drawn soft. Loud music with no
steady kick to carry it is drawn faster and brighter, moves with every rise
heard, and shakes on a hit. A plan whose level stays far from what is heard is
dropped until the two agree.

**Without a plan.** The live listener takes its beat from kicks alone, glides
its phase, and reports no tempo for music without a steady beat.

**The shape of the music, and landscapes.** Tension, release, hush and a
slow breath move the light. Four landscapes are drawn in their own colours,
with nothing bent over them.

**A flash limit.** The light of each of six regions of the picture is held
near a slow reference.

**Tracks carried by their arrangement.** A track whose kicks are light, by
the median peak of the narrow kick band on the beats that carry one, is
carried by its arrangement and not its beat. Every other track is planned as
described above. The analyser measures one more thing for this: the level of
the difference between the left and right channels, which says how wide the
sound is.

The plan takes the texture of such a track every quarter of a second: its
level, the balance of its five bands, its brightness and its width. A
boundary is where the texture on the two sides differs, over four seconds and
over twelve, and it is placed on the sharpest change near it. Its strength is
how far the half minute after it differs from the half minute before it, plus
the step in level across it. A strong boundary is major. Where the sound
stops is found separately, and what follows is silent. Each part has an
intensity, from its level against the track's quiet and loud levels, and a
heaviness, from how loud, dense and steady it is.

A major boundary is cut to on the frame nearest it, and a hard one at once. A
lesser boundary changes the scene by degrees. The mood, the heaviness, the
light and the speed of the picture are the part's. A kick lands as strong as
its part is intense. After the stop the picture is dark and still.

One threshold on level was rejected as the way to find sections, because
sections of this music differ at the same level.

## How it was checked

`make check` passes. Beyond the unit tests, the whole chain was replayed
frame by frame on eight real tracks, which are not in the repository: the
real analyser, the plan it built, live readings rebuilt from the same audio,
and the visualiser.

| Track kind | Beats with a kick pulse on the beat frame | Tempo |
|---|---|---|
| House, 128 BPM | 93–100% in drummed sections, 0% in the breakdown | 128 |
| Techno, 125 BPM | 100% | 125 |
| Swing jazz | 99–100% | 137 |
| Two tracks without a beat | none | none |

Against the kick band of those tracks the beat grid sits within 4 ms. On
rendered frames no region flashes more than 1.5 times a second across the
stress cases, against 4 to 7 before the limit.

The person who asked for this judged each build by eye and ear against the
music, and accepted the last one.

For tracks carried by their arrangement, a map of one long song with many
sections was made outside the program, from published descriptions and from
measuring the recording, and confirmed by a listener who knows it. The map
scores a run (`TestReplayAgainstAMap`, behind the `replay` build tag) and is
never read by the program. Against it the build cuts at all five scene
changes and nowhere else, four of them on the frame.

## What went wrong on the way

Three parts were built in parallel and each passed its own tests. Put
together, they were worse than the release before: the kick pulse came a
frame early about half the time, a breakdown pulsed as if it had kicks, and
the flash limit as first built slowed every cut. None of this showed until
the whole chain was replayed on a real track and measured beat by beat. The
replay is now in the tree behind the `replay` build tag, and `make vet`
keeps it compiling.

## Not covered

- Other songs carried by their arrangement were run and not judged by a
  listener. The build cuts too often on some quiet songs, and misses a change
  of section that comes without a change in level.
- The flash limit is measured for full colour on a dark terminal background.
  It does not cover a light background, a terminal that drops colour, flashes
  smaller than a sixth of the picture, or the saturated-red rule.
- The plan is aligned to playback for files played as they are. A track the
  server transcodes was not measured.
- The first seconds of a track, before its plan arrives, use the live
  listener.
